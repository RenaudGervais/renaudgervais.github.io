Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This OAuth client is used only by its owner to access their own Gmail accounts through the open-source terminal email client Matcha.

  • The app accesses Gmail over IMAP and SMTP solely to read, organize, and send the owner's own email.
  • OAuth tokens and email data are stored only on the owner's local device.
  • No data is collected by, transmitted to, or stored on any server operated by the developer, and nothing is shared with third parties.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training any model.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
